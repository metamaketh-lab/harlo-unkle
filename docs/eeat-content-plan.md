## Schedule

- **Cadence:** 2 articles per week (Wednesday + Saturday)
- **Duration:** ~12 weeks to complete initial batch
- **Refresh:** Update oldest articles monthly with new pricing

## Article Format

- 1,500-2,000 words
- Author byline: "Harlo Unkle Editorial Team"
- Real SGD pricing with ranges
- HDB/condo/landed references
- FAQ section (3-7 questions)
- Internal links to service pages (3-5 minimum)
- Singapore regulations where relevant
- Natural, conversational tone
- Minimal Singlish (max 1 per article)

## Anti-AI Measures

- Varied article structures (not identical templates)
- First-person experience sentences
- Specific local details (named estates, MRT stations)
- Mixed article types (rotate cost guides, how-tos, comparisons)
- Human polish pass before publishing
- No `{{VAR}}` or `$VARIABLE` leftover in HTML
