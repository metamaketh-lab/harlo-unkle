Digital Lock Singapore: Best Models, Cost, and Installation

A practical comparison of digital locks for HDB homes — compatibility, costs, fire-rated rules, and which features are worth paying for.

Digital locks have gone from “nice-to-have” to standard in Singapore HDBs. But this isn’t a simple Amazon buy — your main door is likely fire-rated, your lock body must match the door thickness, and a bad install can void your door’s fire certification. We’ve installed and repaired enough HDB doors to tell you which decisions actually matter.

Best Digital Lock Models for Singapore

These are the models we see holding up after two-plus years of daily family use in HDB estates:

  • Schlage Encode Plus: Reliable, solid build, easy app management. Good fit for standard HDB doors.
  • Yale YRD226: Proven track record, widely supported by local locksmiths, spare parts easy to find.
  • Danalock V3: Retrofits well if you want to keep your existing deadbolt body.
  • Philips Alpha: Good fingerprint recognition, simpler install. Budget option with decent durability.
  • Samsung SHP-DS38: Sleek, but more sensitive to Singapore humidity over time.

Realistic Costs

  • Digital lock unit: $180-$450
  • Installation: $120-$220
  • Old lock removal and disposal: Usually included
  • Door reinforcement plate: $30-$60 if your door frame is soft

Total job: usually $300-$650 for a straightforward HDB main-door install.

HDB Fire-Rated Door Rules

Most HDB main doors are rated. Once you cut, drill, or replace the lock body incorrectly, you can compromise the certification. That’s why you want someone who knows HDB door cores — hollow metal, solid timber, and fire-rated laminates all behave differently.

Fingerprint vs PIN vs App — What to Choose

For Singapore families, fingerprint + PIN backup is the sweet spot. Kids lose keys; app-only locks are annoying when your phone battery dies. If you rent out, PIN + temporary passcode is cleaner than handing over keys.

Battery Life and Backup

Most locks last 12-18 months. Look for low-battery alerts. Always keep a physical key outside or with a neighbour — and confirm the lock supports emergency USB power from a standard power bank.
