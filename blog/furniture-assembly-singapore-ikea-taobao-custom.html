Furniture Assembly Singapore: IKEA, Taobao, and Custom Builds

Realistic assembly pricing, why Taobao furniture is different, and the times when a handyman is worth the $80.

Furniture assembly looks easy until you’re holding three leftover screws, an upside-down wardrobe panel, and an Allen key at 11pm. In Singapore, where IKEA dominates and Taobao furniture is increasingly common, the assembly experience can vary wildly. Here’s what to expect, what it should cost, and when hiring a handyman makes sense.

Typical Assembly Prices in 2026

  • Small item (stool, side table, shoe rack): $40-$60
  • Medium item (desk, bookshelf, dining table): $60-$120
  • Large wardrobe or kitchen cabinet: $120-$220
  • Multi-item full-room package: $250-$450

IKEA vs Taobao vs Custom

IKEA furniture is engineered to be repeatable. Most competent handymen can assemble it fast. Taobao and custom furniture are less predictable — simplified drawings, inconsistent labelling, and one-off designs that don’t match the manual. Budget an extra 20-30% time buffer for Taobao.

When to Call a Handyman Instead of DIY

  • You’re missing screws or boards and can’t tell from the manual.
  • The item needs wall-mounting in an HDB with mixed wall types.
  • You don’t have the drill, level, or stepladder.
  • The furniture is heavy enough to cause injury if assembled incorrectly.

HDB and Safety Notes

Tall wardrobes and shelves should be wall-anchored. HDBs have different wall compositions — concrete, brick, and partition walls each need the right fastener. A professional handyman brings that knowledge rather than guessing with rawl plugs.
