Water Heater Installation Singapore: Cost, Rules, and Best Options

Instant vs storage heater costs, HDB wall rules, PUB safety requirements, and how to avoid a leak or electrical trip after install.

A bad water heater install in an HDB bathroom isn’t just annoying — it can damage the ceiling below, trip your DB board, or fail PUB inspection. After years of handling leak and heater call-outs, here’s the practical guide we wish more homeowners read before they bought a unit online.

Instant vs Storage Water Heater

Instant heaters heat water on demand. They’re compact, cheaper upfront, and ideal for 1-2 person households. Expect $300-$700 for the unit plus $180-$350 install.

Storage heaters keep a tank of hot water. Better for families, longer showers, and back-to-back usage. Expect $500-$1,200 for the unit plus $250-$450 install.

2026 Installation Costs

  • Instant heater install: $180-$350
  • Storage heater install (standard height): $250-$450
  • Extra pipe-rerouting / false-ceiling work: $200-$600
  • Gas heater install (if bay gas connection exists): $300-$500

Rules That Matter in HDB

  • Wall strength: Storage heaters are heavy. Some older HDB bathrooms need reinforcement brackets.
  • Electrical circuit: HDB heaters should have a dedicated circuit with an isolator switch.
  • Pipe routing: Concealed piping is cleaner but harder to repair later. Exposed piping is fine if neatly installed.
  • Gas heaters: If your flat doesn’t have a gas bay pipe, you can’t install a gas heater without major rerouting.

PUB and Safety Compliance

PUB’s water-heater safety notices focus on temperature control, electrical earthing, and secure mounting. Ask your installer for the compliance sticker. Without it, your insurance claim may be contested after a water leak.

How to Avoid Post-Install Leaks

Most heater leaks come from bad pipe joints, missing Teflon tape, or overtightened connections that crack over time. Insist on visible pipe supports, leak testing before closing up, and a 30-day callback.
