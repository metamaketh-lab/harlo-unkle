Handyman vs Renovation Contractor: When to Hire Which in Singapore

Stop overpaying contractors for small HDB jobs. Clear guide to knowing when a handyman is the smarter hire — and when you need a full contractor.

One of the most common confusions in Singapore home services: when do I need a handyman, and when do I need a renovation contractor? Getting this wrong can mean overspending by hundreds or even thousands of dollars.

What a Handyman Covers

Handymen handle small to medium repairs and installations that don’t require structural changes:

  • Wall-mounting TVs, shelves, cabinets
  • Furniture assembly and minor carpentry
  • Door lock replacement and hinge fixes
  • Plumbing small jobs (tap replacement, minor leaks)
  • Electrical small jobs (socket replacement, light fixture swap)

Typical rate: $40–$80/hour or $80–$220 per small task.

When You Need a Contractor

Bring in a renovation contractor when the work involves HDB approval, structural changes, or multiple trades at once:

  • Whole flat renovation / resale HDB Reno
  • Kitchen or toilet hacking and re-tiling
  • Electrical rewiring
  • Plumbing relaying / wet area expansion
  • Any work likely to affect neighbours or building structure

HDB Approval: The Hidden Cost

Some tasks that seem simple — relocating a toilet, hacking walls, installing new pipework — require HDB Renovation Permit. Contractors usually handle the paperwork. Handymen typically don’t. Work without approval risks rectification orders or penalties.

The Overlap Zone

Aircon installation, full false-ceiling work, and built-in carpentry sit in a grey area. Some handymen offer them; others subcontract. Always clarify who is doing the work and whether they’re licensed.
