Door Repair & Lock Replacement: HDB Residents Need to Know

Digital lock installations, gate repairs, HDB fire-rated door rules, lock replacement costs — practical guidance for Singapore homes.

Doors and locks in HDB estates have more rules than people realise. Here’s what matters before you book a repair or upgrade.

HDB Fire-Rated Door Rules

Main doors in HDB flats must be fire-rated (min. 1 hour) or have valid certification. Replacing with a non-fire-rated door risks your insurance and neighbour safety. Always verify the label on the replacement door.

Digital Lock Installation Checklist

  • Confirm lock thickness and bevel compatibility
  • Check bore hole distance (usually 60mm or 70mm)
  • Make sure the door isn’t warped (digital locks can’t compensate)
  • Ask about cylinder override — vital for power failure
  • Choose a brand with SG warranty and local service

Gate vs Main Door

Gate repairs are usually simpler — hinges, latches, and frame alignment. Main door work can be invasive and usually requires the whole door removed. Factor in access time and whether the installer will bring helpers.

Costs

  • Standard lock replacement: $120–$250
  • Digital lock installation (unit only): $250–$500
  • Gate lock repair/install: $100–$220
