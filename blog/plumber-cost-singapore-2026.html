How Much Does a Plumber Cost in Singapore in 2026? (HDB vs Condo Pricing)

Real 2026 pricing for common plumbing jobs — leaky taps, pipe relaying, HDB vs condo rates, and when to call a pro vs DIY.

“How much does a plumber cost in Singapore?” is one of the most searched home-services questions on the island — and the honest answer is: it depends on the job, the HDB vs condo context, and whether it’s an emergency.

Common Plumbing Jobs and Realistic 2026 Pricing

Below are typical price bands for Singapore. These are based on actual provider quotes, not advertised “from” prices that hide call-out fees.

  • Leaky tap / tap replacement: $80–$180
  • Toilet unblock / fix: $100–$180
  • Pipe leak repair: $150–$350
  • Water heater installation/replacement: $180–$380
  • Pipe relaying (HDB whole flat): $1,800–$4,500
  • Bathroom fittings install: $250–$600

HDB vs Condo: Why the Difference

HDB flats usually have standardised pipework, making diagnosis faster. Condos often have longer access routes, management approval steps, and stricter working-hour rules — so expect a 10–25% premium for the same job.

Emergency vs Standard Call-Out

Burst pipes at midnight cost more than a scheduled morning visit. Emergency rates typically include a surge premium of $80–$150. If it’s not life-safety critical, booking a standard slot usually saves money.

How to Avoid Surprise Bills

  1. Ask for a fixed quote before work starts.
  2. Confirm whether parts are included or extra.
  3. For pipe relaying, get 2–3 site assessments.
  4. Avoid “per hour” pricing for big jobs; prefer lump-sum quotes.

Red Flags in Plumber Pricing

Watch out for quotes that are 30%+ below market — often a sign of reused parts or rushed work. For HDB, also verify the provider has the right approach to HDB pipe standards; non-compliant relaying can fail inspection.

When DIY Actually Makes Sense

Replacing a washer on a tap or unblocking a surface drain is usually fine to DIY. Pipe relaying, gas-related fittings, and anything involving the main line should go to a licensed plumber.
