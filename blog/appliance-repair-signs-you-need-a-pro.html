Appliance Repair Singapore: 5 Fridge & Washer Warning Signs

Know when to repair vs replace — fridge, washing machine, oven warning signs, realistic repair cost ranges, and when a pro is worth it.

Before you dump a 4-year-old fridge or washing machine, run through this list. Many “broken” appliances are fixed with one well-targeted repair — saving you hundreds.

Fridge Warning Signs

  • Mutating temperature: fridge side warm but freezer cold? Likely sealed-system fault.
  • Condensation on outside: door seal issue, cheap fix.
  • Motor running non-stop: thermostat or dirty condenser.
  • Water pooling inside: drainage blockage or defrost fault.

Washing Machine Warning Signs

  • Not draining: pump or filter blockage.
  • Vibration and noise: often shock-absorbers or drum bearings.
  • Leaks from bottom: door seal or inlet valve.
  • Spin not reaching full speed: motor coupling or load balance.

Repair Cost Benchmarks

  • Diagnostic visit: $80–$150
  • Seal / filter / valve replacement: $120–$280
  • Motor / pump replacement: $250–$500
  • Sealed-system repair: $400–$700

Replace When:

  • Machine is 10+ years old
  • Repair estimate >50% of replacement cost
  • Multiple faults appearing at once
  • Inverter board / main PCB failure on budget models
