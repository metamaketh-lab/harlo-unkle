How to Vet a Home Services Provider in Singapore: 7 Questions

Before you book any handyman, plumber, or aircon tech in Singapore — ask these 7 questions to filter unqualified providers and avoid surprise bills.

Singapore’s home services market has great professionals — and operators who overpromise and under-deliver. These 7 questions filter the best from the rest.

7 Questions to Ask Before Hiring

  1. Are you licensed/registered for this specific trade? Electrical needs EMA; plumbing may need PUB-related certs for larger jobs.
  2. Is this a fixed quote or estimate? Favour fixed quotes for transparent budgeting.
  3. Do you include materials, or do I pay separately? Clarify before the job starts.
  4. What’s your warranty? 30–90 days is standard; less is suspicious.
  5. Can you provide a reference from someone in my estate type? HDB vs condo experience differs.
  6. How long should the job take? Vague timelines are a bad sign.
  7. Do you have insurance? For jobs where damage is possible (drilling, wiring), ask.

Yellow Flags to Walk Away From

  • Pressure to pay cash for a “better price”
  • Unwillingness to itemise charges
  • No physical company name or contact number
  • Quotes half the going rate with vague scope

Why This Matters

A bad hire doesn’t just waste money. In HDB flats, poorly done electrical or waterproofing work can create fire or flood risk across units. Choosing qualified providers protects your home and your neighbours.
